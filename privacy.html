DueBack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

DueBack is designed to be private by design. Everything you track — purchases, receipt photos, return deadlines and refunds — is stored only on your iPhone. There is no account, no DueBack server, and we cannot see your data.

What is collected

  • Your items and settings: stored on-device only (SwiftData). Receipt and order photos you attach stay on the device, and text recognition runs on-device (Apple Vision) — photos are never uploaded. Deleted permanently when you use "Delete all my data" in Settings or delete the app.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are processed by Apple and managed with RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives purchase receipts and an anonymous app user ID to unlock Pro. No purchase data is linked to your identity by us.

What is NOT collected

No analytics SDKs, no advertising trackers, no location, no contacts, no email or store-account access, and your data is never uploaded or sold.

Retailer policies

Return windows shown in the app are starting estimates for convenience. DueBack is not affiliated with any retailer and does not guarantee any store's policy — always verify with your receipt and the retailer.
