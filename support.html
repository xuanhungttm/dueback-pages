FAQ

How does DueBack know my return deadline? When you add a purchase, DueBack suggests the return-by date from its built-in policy library (17 major US retailers, including shorter electronics windows and holiday extensions). You can always pick a different date — yours wins — and you can adjust any retailer's window in the Retailers tab.

A deadline passed. Why didn't the item mark itself as missed? On purpose. DueBack never guesses: it asks whether you kept the item or missed the window, and only counts a loss when you confirm it.

My refund hasn't arrived. Open the item: once a store has had your return for 7 days with no refund recorded, DueBack shows a checklist (check the original payment method, search email and spam, open the retailer's returns help). Pro also sends refund-overdue alerts at 7 and 14 days.

The receipt scan filled something in wrong. Scanning only pre-fills the form — check every field before saving. Nothing is ever saved automatically.

What's free vs Pro? Free tracks 3 active returns with the full policy library, deadline reminders, receipt scanning and a 30-day money ledger. Pro unlocks unlimited returns, the full Refund Loop with overdue alerts, full ledger history and CSV export.

Restore a purchase: Settings → Restore Purchases.
